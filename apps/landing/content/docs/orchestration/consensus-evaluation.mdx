## Evaluate answers, not confidence

`DebateRunner` and `consensusMerge` accept a synchronous `consensusEvaluator`
that reads actual `SubAgentResult` responses and returns a finite score in `[0, 1]`.
Similar confidence scores do not establish agreement.

Without an evaluator, with fewer than two participants, or if any participant failed,
the score is `0` (unknown). A debate then exhausts its round budget and returns
`requiresHumanReview: true`. A positive score meeting `consensusThreshold` permits early exit.
Evaluator errors and invalid scores propagate rather than claiming agreement.

For agents returning a single canonical decision label:

```typescript
const debate = new DebateRunner(agentRunner, {
  consensusEvaluator: (results) =>
    results.every((r) => r.response.trim() === results[0].response.trim()) ? 1 : 0,
});
```

For prose or structured findings, provide a domain-specific evaluator. Agreement
is not proof of correctness; evaluate quality separately. The highest-scored
successful response remains the selected answer, even when human review is required.
