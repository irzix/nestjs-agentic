## Why Multi-Agent Debate?

Single models suffer from confirmation bias. When multiple agents review a complex artifact independently, disagreements often emerge (e.g. Security says "BLOCK", Performance says "OPTIMAL").

`DebateRoundRunner` conducts structured debate rounds:

1. **Round 1 (Independent Assessment)**: Specialists produce baseline scores and findings.
2. **Variance Check**: If assessment scores diverge significantly (variance exceeding configured threshold), Round 2 is triggered.
3. **Round 2 (Cross-Examination)**: Each agent receives the peer reviews of the other specialists and must either defend, modify, or concede their score.
4. **Synthesis**: The lead synthesizer reconciles the final consensus.
